Looking up the latest release…

On first launch, macOS asks you to allow Jauge's background service in System Settings > General > Login Items. That service is what talks to the battery.

Features

Keeping a battery between 50 and 80% slows its wear. Jauge does it for you, even when the app is closed.

Charge limit

Pick a limit from 20 to 100%. On macOS 26 and later, the Mac's own charge limit does the work from 80%.

Discharge

Run from the battery while plugged in, down to your limit. Start it by hand or let it start on its own.

Idle range

Recharge only once the level drops below a range, instead of topping up after every percent.

Calibration

A full cycle: 100%, down to 10%, back to 100%, one hour of rest, then your limit.

Heat protection

Charging pauses above the temperature you set, checked in five-minute windows.

Power flow

Watts from the adapter and the battery to the Mac, live. Warns you when the adapter is too weak.

Schedule

Set a limit, calibrate, charge to 100% or toggle Low Power Mode at set times. Missed tasks can run on wake.

Shortcuts and Terminal

Every action in the Shortcuts app, and jaugectl on the command line.

And also

  • Charge to 100% once
  • MagSafe LED colors
  • Stay awake until the limit
  • Freeze the level during sleep
  • Hardware battery percentage
  • Works for every user account

Privacy

Jauge only goes online to check for updates on GitHub. No account, no telemetry, nothing leaves your Mac.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Jauge need a background service?

Changing how the battery charges requires administrator rights. A small service runs as root and does only that; the menu bar app talks to it through a local socket that only administrators can use. You approve it once in System Settings.

Which Macs are supported?

MacBooks with Apple silicon, on macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.

How does a limit below 80% work on macOS 26 and later?

macOS's built-in limit stops at 80%. Below that, Jauge switches between battery and adapter around your limit, so the level moves by about 1%.

How do updates work?

Jauge checks the latest GitHub release on startup, then every 6 hours. Each update is signed: the app checks the signature, installs it and restarts its service.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Jauge and move it to the Trash. With Homebrew: brew uninstall --zap jauge.
